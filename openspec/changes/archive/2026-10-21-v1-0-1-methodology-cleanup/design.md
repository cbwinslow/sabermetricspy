# Design

Evidence first: each claim in the audit was re-measured by running the code against the recorded fixtures; claims that rested on memory were checked against the pages they cite (Baseball-Reference, FanGraphs, baseballr). Where a page could not be read (Savant glossary, B-Ref pitching glossary) the item stays on the research list in `docs/VERIFICATION.md` instead of being guessed.

Alternatives are in ADR-0061 and ADR-0062.
